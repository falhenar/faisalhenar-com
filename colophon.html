How this site is made

I am not a web developer. What this site holds, how it looks, and how it is arranged are my choices. The code that makes it work was written with AI tools, mainly Claude and ChatGPT. Along the way I asked them for suggestions and took some of them.

The personal writing is my own: A note from me, the reflections, and what I write about books, talks and places. I sometimes ask an AI tool to read a draft and comment on structure or wording, much as I might ask a friend. Smaller texts, such as image descriptions and some of the Dutch page text, were drafted with AI and checked by me.

The photographs are my own. AI is not used to create or alter them.

Every recommendation on this site comes from my own reading, listening and practice. I sometimes use AI to check a detail against its source, such as a sutta reference or a date.
