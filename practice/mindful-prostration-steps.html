The prostration, step by step

Also available in Dutch

Every position in one prostration, from kneeling to standing, with the word noted at each movement.

Companion to sheet 3 · Download PDF · Download PDF (Nederlands)

This is the same sequence as the mindful prostration sheet, shown position by position. The sheet explains what the practice is for and how to note; this page is for checking the shape of a movement when the words are not enough.

The word under each drawing is the note said silently while the movement is made, once as it begins, once in the middle, once as it ends. Numbers follow the sequence from the first kneeling to standing up again.

Kneeling

  • A toes tucked under
  • B or sitting on the feet

Either position is correct. Choose the one your knees and ankles allow.

Going down

  • 1 sitting
  • 2 turning
  • 3 raising
  • 4 touching
  • 5 turning
  • 6 raising
  • 7 touching
  • 8 raising
  • 9 touching
  • 10 lowering
  • 11 touching
  • 12 bending
  • 13 lowering
  • 14 touching
  • 15 covering
  • 16 lowering
  • 17 touching
  • 18 covering
  • 19 bending
  • 20 touching

Coming up

  • 21 raising
  • 22 turning
  • 23 raising
  • 24 touching
  • 25 turning
  • 26 raising
  • 27 touching
  • 28 raising
  • 29 touching
  • 30 lowering
  • 31 touching

Repeat 12 to 31 twice more, so there are three prostrations in all, then continue with 32.

Finishing

  • 32 lowering
  • 33 touching
  • 34 covering
  • 35 lowering
  • 36 touching
  • 37 covering
  • 38 sitting
  • 39 standing

These instructions follow the meditation manual of Yuttadhammo Bhikkhu. The technique is the style of insight practice as taught by Ajahn Tong Sirimangalo, in which experience is noted silently and in plain language as it occurs.
