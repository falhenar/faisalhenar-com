The same timer as the Android app, as a page you can use straight from a desktop browser: duration, stages, and bells for the noting practice on this page.

This runs entirely in the tab: nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is saved except what you choose to save as a preset, on this device. Unlike the app, it needs the tab to stay open and in the foreground to keep time, closing it stops the sit.
