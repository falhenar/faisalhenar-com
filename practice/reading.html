Reading

A few books worth sitting with slowly, a few reflections at a time rather than straight through.

The Four Noble Truths

Ajahn Sumedho, illustrated edition with drawings by Ajahn Sucitto, published 2020

A short, plain account of the Buddha's central teaching: that suffering has a cause, and an end.

Read online →

Everything Is Teaching Us

Ajahn Chah, translated by Paul Breiter, reprinted for his centenary in 2018

Talks on meeting ordinary situations — irritation, boredom, doubt — as material for practice rather than obstacles to it.

Read online →

The Worn-out Skin

Nyanaponika Thera, The Wheel No. 241, Buddhist Publication Society, 1977

A translation and short commentary on the Uraga Sutta, on shedding attachment the way a snake sheds its skin.
