Watching

Buddhist channels and recorded talks, arranged from a first look to sitting in.

Video earns its place when there is something to see: a hand position, a face working through a question, the ordinary business of a monastery. Where that is not true, the same teacher is usually better on Listening, and several of these appear in both rooms.

The sections run from a first look to the least introductory. That ordering is about how much is asked of you, not about lineage, so a Tibetan curriculum sits near the top and a forest monastery near the bottom. Everything here is on YouTube and carries its advertising. Where a teacher or monastery keeps the same material on its own site, the entry says so.
