Reflections

A slow read through the texts I keep returning to, published as I actually get to them rather than on a schedule. The suttas below largely follow the structure of Bhikkhu Bodhi's anthology In the Buddha's Words; a handful after it don't, they're simply ones that stayed with me. Sources and translations are noted per entry.
