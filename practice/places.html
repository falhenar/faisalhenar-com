Teachers

Ajahn Tong Sirimangalo

Former abbot, Wat Chom Tong, Chiang Mai

Read a short bio

Ajahn Tong Sirimangalo was born in 1923 and ordained as a novice in 1934, receiving full ordination a decade later. In the 1950s he was sent by the Northern Thai Sangha to Wat Mahathat in Bangkok to study the four foundations of mindfulness, followed by two years teaching and practicing in Burma alongside the Venerable Mahasi Sayadaw. He went on to become a widely respected meditation teacher in Thailand, leading Vipassana courses across Europe and America from the early 1980s onward. Most of his life and teaching was based at Wat Phradhatu Sri Chom Tong, a large, well-known meditation monastery near Chiang Mai, which he headed as abbot and which includes an international center for foreign practitioners.

Ajahn Poonsak Suwadhano

Whom I consider my primary teacher

Read a short bio

A disciple of Ajahn Tong Sirimangalo, Ajahn Poonsak taught me during a 40-day retreat at Wat Chom Tong in 2023. His teaching style is patient and unhurried: attentive to where I actually was in my practice, rather than a fixed curriculum. May whatever merit I make in my practice be dedicated to him.

Places I've Practiced

Wat Chom Tong ↗

Ajahn Tong Sirimangalo's lineage · Chiang Mai, Thailand

Founded in 1991, teaching the four foundations of mindfulness through sitting, walking, and prostration practice. Courses open to both Thai and international students, with no set fee: you give dana, a donation, at the end of your stay, whatever your means allow. Home to Ajahn Tong Sirimangalo as the late abbot, and to Ajahn Poonsak Suwadhano, whom I consider my primary teacher. This is where I sat my longest retreat so far, and it changed the course of my life: looking back at where I was a few years ago, the difference is night and day. The meditation instructions on this site follow that same method, taught by Yuttadhammo Bhikkhu.

Casa de Meditación Vipassana ↗

Ajahn Tong lineage · Mexico City, Mexico

Theravada meditation in the northern Thai lineage, founded by a student of Ajahn Tong Sirimangalo. Workshops, group sits, and silent retreats from 3 to 20 days, in person and online. Unlike most centers in this lineage, retreats here carry a set fee rather than pure donation, since the group doesn't have a permanent space of its own and rents one for each retreat; the fee covers lodging, meals, and parking, though not transport. Teachers from Wat Chom Tong visit regularly; in 2024 I had the chance to practice with Ajahn Poonsak again, when he came over from Thailand.

Mosteiro Suddhavāri ↗

Theravada Forest Tradition · Brazil

Connected to the Sociedade Budista do Brasil, offering teachings, chanting, and meditation guidance. Everything here, including lodging, is free of charge, sustained entirely by voluntary donation. Recorded talks, a digital library, and accommodation for visitors. I stayed here for ten days in 2025 and learned a great deal about carrying mindfulness into daily activities, thanks to Ajahn Mudito and everyone who supports the monastery.

In the Same Lineage

Ajahn Tong Sirimangalo's teaching spread well beyond Wat Chom Tong. These carry the same method elsewhere in the world. I haven't practiced at any of them myself.

Sirimangalo International ↗

Ajahn Tong lineage · Ratnapura, Sri Lanka

Founded by Yuttadhammo Bhikkhu, whose instructions can be found on his site. The meditation centre is in Ratnapura, Sri Lanka.

Wat Chomtong Germany ↗

Ajahn Tong lineage · Morshausen, Germany

A monastery branch built as a memorial to Ajahn Tong Sirimangalo, staffed by monks from Thailand.

Buddhavipassana Meditation Centre ↗

Ajahn Tong lineage · Toronto, Canada

Runs a 15-day basic course and 10-day retreats, taught by Alex and Miriam Young.

Santidhamma Vipassana Meditation Center of Argentina ↗

Ajahn Tong lineage · Buenos Aires, Argentina

Part of the Spanish-speaking Anumodana network, offering courses and sits in the same tradition.

Northern Vipassana, the international department at Wat Chom Tong, keeps a fuller list of branches worldwide: see the branches page ↗.

Where I Started

Vipassana Suriname ↗

S.N. Goenka tradition · Suriname

Local practitioners who organize ten-day Vipassana courses in the S.N. Goenka tradition roughly every other year, usually at a temporary retreat site rather than a permanent center. As with all Goenka courses worldwide, there's no fee: you donate only after completing a course, and only if and what you're able. This is where I did my first retreat, and I'm grateful for that opportunity, though the technique and structure differ somewhat from the tradition I practice in now. They also run a Facebook page.

Also Worth Knowing About

Forest Sangha ↗

Ajahn Chah's lineage · worldwide network

A portal to the branch monasteries that grew out of Ajahn Chah's teaching lineage, with talks, publications, and links to individual monasteries across the world. A few of them, including Amaravati and Cittaviveka, are covered individually on Watching.

Dhammadipa ↗

Mahasi Sayadaw lineage · Amsterdam, Netherlands

Offers courses, sitting groups, and city retreats in the vipassana tradition. Open to newcomers as well as experienced practitioners. Same method as Sayar Myat on the Listening shelf.
