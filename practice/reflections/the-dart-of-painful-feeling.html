The Dart of Painful Feeling

SN 36.6 · Sallasutta

In the Buddha's Words · I.2(1) · 20 August 2026

When an unlearned ordinary person experiences painful physical feelings they sorrow and wail and lament, beating their breast and falling into confusion. They experience two feelings: physical and mental.
It’s like a person who is struck with an arrow, only to be struck with a second arrow. That person experiences the feeling of two arrows.
In the same way, when an unlearned ordinary person experiences painful physical feelings they sorrow and wail and lament, beating their breast and falling into confusion. They experience two feelings: physical and mental.

We will all experience suffering in our lives in one form or another. In that sense, getting struck by the "first arrow" is unavoidable. We age, we get sick and, at some point, we will all die. Along the way there will inevitably be painful and uncomfortable feelings.

The "second arrow" took me a while to comprehend. It seems easy to tell ourselves not to wail and lament over the pain we are feeling, only to catch ourselves wailing and lamenting again a few minutes later. It is not about telling ourselves to "toughen up" or "get over it already". That can become its own form of struggling with what we feel. Nor is it much help to tell someone else to "get a grip" or "lighten up". We can only begin to understand this through our own investigation.

Mindfulness gives us a way to make that investigation. We learn to know when we are walking, standing, sitting or lying down. These are the four postures to be mindful of, but that knowing can extend throughout our experience: knowing when we are touching, eating, talking or feeling. In the vipassana practice I was taught, I began to understand this less as an idea and more as something observable. When an unpleasant feeling arises, I can sometimes see not only the feeling itself, but also what the mind starts doing with it. That is where the second arrow becomes something I can actually recognize.

I am not saying that I am completely free from suffering. What I can say is that life has become easier since I have been able to put down the second arrow sooner than I could before.
