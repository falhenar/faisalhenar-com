No I there to be found

From practice · Suddhavari Monastery, São Lourenço, Brazil · 22 October 2026

In November 2025, I had the opportunity to spend time at Suddhavari Monastery in São Lourenço, Brazil. In the presence of Ajahn Mudito, a few resident monks, and members of the local lay community I was blessed to witness the Sangha this close to home.

It was not an organized retreat, so for this period I was honoured to just be part of the community. Even though there was a language barrier, we had meals together, worked together, and talked about Dhamma. And most importantly, there was plenty of time to practice.

I always find retreats hard to describe. But after a few days being there, I wrote a short poem.

No I there to be found As far as the eye can see no I there to be found So much the ear captures yet the I makes no sound Smelling, tasting, touching worldly misery and worldly pleasure Lifetime after lifetime in this samsaric round Perceiving and concocting an I that keeps us bound Seeing death to this awareness another birth will be around Seeing the nature of existence the deathless can be found ​Wednesday, November 26, 2025 Suddhavari Monastery, Brazil
