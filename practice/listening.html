Listening

Podcasts and narrated collections I keep coming back to, most of them from the Thai forest tradition.

These are ongoing feeds rather than finished works, so the useful question is not which is best but what kind of listening each one is. A few are books read aloud, with a beginning and an end. Most are archives you dip into. The sections below are sorted that way.

If you want somewhere to start: the evening talks from Metta Forest Monastery are about twelve minutes each and ask little of you, and the Ajahn Chah audiobook will keep you occupied for months. Everything here is free. One entry carries advertising and says so.

Texts read aloud

Finite works, narrated. These have a first episode and a last one, so you can work through them the way you would a book.

From the monasteries

Places rather than single voices. Talks given to whoever was sitting there that evening, recorded as they happened.

Single teachers

One voice at length. The division from the section above is loose: these feeds come from monasteries too, they are just gathered under a teacher's name.

The insight tradition

Outside the Thai forest lineage most of this page sits in. Both come by way of Mahasi Sayadaw and the Burmese satipaṭṭhāna teaching that reached the West through him.
