A personal note

I've benefited a great deal from the teachers I've met along this path, particularly the instructions I received from Ajahn Poonsak at Wat Chom Tong, in the lineage of Ajahn Tong. If you're serious about deepening your practice…

Continue readingShow less

I'd recommend finding a teacher who can guide you. We live in a time when information is readily available online, which is genuinely useful, but it doesn't compare to practicing under an established teacher.

That said, sometimes circumstances don't allow for practicing with a teacher. In those cases, texts and meditation apps can be a good way to learn, though it takes real effort and determination on your own. It also helps to find others willing to talk, share, and practice together. If you'd ever like to talk about practice, feel free to reach out through the contact page.
