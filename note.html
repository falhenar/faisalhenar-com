Practice, photography, and a life in progress.

Welcome to my small corner of the web. My name is Faisal.

I am trying to walk the path of Buddhism as best I can, as a lay practitioner living an ordinary life. For me, this path is not separate from daily life, but something expressed through the way I meet others, the way I work, and the way I try to be present.

I also started a small Facebook community, Boeddhisme in Suriname, out of curiosity about who else nearby shared this interest. It has grown since then through that same shared curiosity, and stays open to anyone in Suriname curious about Buddhism, whether you've been practicing for years or are just starting to look into it.

A large part of my life is shared with my wife, Daisy. Her kindness, patience, and support make much of what I do possible, and I would like this quiet place on the internet to be an expression of my gratitude for her presence in my life.

Besides my practice, I am an amateur photographer. Through this website I hope to share some of my photographs and the moments that caught my attention. I do not see photography merely as creating beautiful images, but as an opportunity to slow down, observe, and appreciate the lives and environments of fellow human beings.

In my professional life, I am grateful to be able to offer my time and effort to a suicide prevention hotline. Listening to people during some of the most difficult moments of their lives continually reminds me of what truly matters and puts my own life into perspective.

This site was built with the help of AI tools, for the code and as a second reader on structure and wording. The photographs are mine, and so is the writing that speaks in my own voice. Where a page summarizes someone else's life or work, I've checked it against the sources.

I hope you enjoy your time here. If something you see or read resonates with you, please feel free to reach out by email.

May we all be free from suffering and find peace and happiness.
